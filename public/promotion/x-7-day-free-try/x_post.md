## 💡 Should You Announce Again After the Launch Tweet?

**Yes, 100%. Here is why:**
1. **The 2% Shelf-Life Reality**: On X, an average tweet is seen by only 2–5% of your followers and almost zero non-followers. One launch tweet is never "the announcement" — social media feeds require regular, story-driven updates.
2. **The Friction Shift (The Real Breakthrough)**: A launch post asking people to pay for an unknown app upfront has high friction. A **7-Day Free Trial (with zero subscription traps)** removes the #1 barrier to download. For 90% of potential users, *this* is the update they can actually act on.
3. **Momentum Signals**: Accounts that only post once look abandoned. Accounts that post *"Update: listened to early feedback and shipped a 7-day free trial"* look credible, alive, and actively maintained.
4. **How to Frame It**: Don't say "Hey buy my app again." Say: *"Update based on early feedback: Movtrak is now completely free to test for 7 days. Roast it in the gym this week!"*

## 🎬 The 5–8 Second Video Hook Blueprint (Highest Algorithmic Reach)

If your account has few followers, **video is your single biggest algorithmic lever**. X heavily pushes short, high-retention vertical videos to non-followers on the "For You" feed.

### The Shot Breakdown (Total Duration: 6–8 seconds)
- **0:00 – 0:02**: Quick shot of an iPhone propped on a chalk bag / floor mat with the recording button tapped.
- **0:02 – 0:05**: Screen recording cut: A climber or lifter moves dynamically; the green bounding box locks on and the digital camera view smoothly pans/reframes to keep them perfectly centered.
- **0:05 – 0:07**: Quick text overlay: *"No gimbal. 100% on-device AI. 7-Day Free Trial on App Store."*

> 💡 **Why this works**: Under 8 seconds means users often watch it 1.5–2 times while reading your tweet text. A completion rate > 100% is the primary signal X uses to blast a video out to non-followers.

## 🚀 Cold-Start Distribution Playbook (For Accounts with Low Followers)

When you have a small following, you cannot rely on followers retweeting you. You must actively tap into existing discovery surfaces.

### Tactic 1: The "Search & Intercept" Technique
Search for people on X who are experiencing the exact pain point right now. Search these queries weekly:
- `"filming myself bouldering"`
- `"climbing video" "out of frame"`
- `"gym tripod" OR "phone gimbal" recommend`
- `"record my lifts" alone`
- `"iPhone gimbal" gym`

**How to reply authentically (Never spam links directly!):**
> *"I had this exact problem every time I climbed solo — got sick of lugging a gimbal around so I ended up building an iOS app (Movtrak) that tracks your body with local AI. Just put out a 7-day free trial if you ever want to test it out without buying gear!"*

### Tactic 2: Leverage X Communities (Bypass Follower Count)
In X Communities, your post is distributed directly to member feeds regardless of your follower count.
Join and share your update in:
- **Rock Climbing & Bouldering Communities** (Focus on the solo-filming pain point).
- **iOS Developers / SwiftUI Communities** (Focus on on-device Vision/CoreML performance).
- **Indie Hackers & Build in Public** (Focus on the zero-subscription lifetime model and shipping fast).

### Tactic 3: The 30-Minute Algorithmic Warm-up
- **15 minutes before posting**: Like, repost, and leave genuine replies on 5–10 recent posts from people in your niche (climbers, indie hackers). This tells X's algorithm that your account is active and primed for engagement.
- **Immediately after posting**: Keep the app open for 30 minutes. Reply to any comment within 5–10 minutes. Early comment velocity is the #1 metric for reaching the "For You" tab.

### Tactic 4: Where to Put the Link
- **The X Link Penalty**: X actively downranks tweets containing external URLs to keep users inside the app.
- **Best Practice for Single Tweets**: Leave the link out of the main post and write: *"Link in the first reply 👇"*. Then post the App Store link as the 1st reply within 10 seconds.
- **Best Practice for Threads**: Place the link in the final tweet (Tweet 5). This avoids the penalty on Tweet 1!
